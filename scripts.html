How to Keep It Always Docked in After Effects

1. macOS Installation Path

Copy CC_Comp_Duplicator.jsx into:
/Applications/Adobe After Effects [Year]/Scripts/ScriptUI Panels/

2. Windows Installation Path

Copy CC_Comp_Duplicator.jsx into:
C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects [Year]\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\

3. Enable Scripting Permissions

In After Effects, go to Settings > Scripting & Expressions and enable "Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network".

4. Dock & Save Workspace

Restart After Effects, open Window > CC_Comp_Duplicator.jsx, drag the panel into your UI, and save your workspace so it opens automatically on every launch.
