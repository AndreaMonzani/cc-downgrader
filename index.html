TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Free Premiere Pro & After Effects Project Downgrader

Convert newer Adobe Creative Cloud projects (.prproj, .aep, .aepx) to older versions online without installing plugins or uploading client assets.

01 • GZIP / XML SCHEMA

Premiere Pro (.prproj)

Decompresses the GZIP XML binary of your .prproj file and patches internal Version attributes for backward compatibility across CC 2023–2027.

02 • RIFX / HEAD CHUNK

After Effects (.aep / .aepx)

Navigates the RIFX binary chunk hierarchy to patch version signatures directly in memory without corrupting compositions, layers, or expressions.

03 • ZERO-UPLOAD SANDBOX

Client-Side Privacy

All stream decompression and binary patching execute locally in your browser sandbox. NDA-protected client projects never leave your workstation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I downgrade a Premiere Pro project?

Drag and drop your .prproj file into the dropzone above. CC Downgrader patches the project schema version and immediately downloads a compatible file ready to open in older Premiere Pro versions.

Can I open a newer project in an older version of After Effects?

Yes. CC Downgrader modifies the internal version headers in .aep and .aepx files so that older After Effects releases open them cleanly.

Is there a free Premiere downgrader tool?

Yes, CC Downgrader is a 100% free, open-source online utility that allows you to downgrade Premiere Pro (.prproj) files without installing any software or creating an account.

How to save Premiere project for older version?

Since Adobe doesn't natively allow exporting to older major versions, drop your saved file into our converter above to automatically generate a legacy-compatible project file.

Is this tool free and safe?

Yes, CC Downgrader is 100% free and MIT-licensed open-source. Because conversions are handled entirely in your browser, zero project data is uploaded or stored on external servers.

Which Adobe Creative Cloud versions are supported?

CC Downgrader supports all modern Adobe Premiere Pro (.prproj) and After Effects (.aep, .aepx) releases including CC 2027, 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, and legacy versions.
