Frequently Asked Questions

How do I downgrade a Premiere Pro project?

Drag and drop your .prproj file into the dropzone above. CC Downgrader will patch the project schema version and immediately download a compatible version ready to open in older Premiere Pro versions.

Can I open a newer project in an older version of After Effects?

Yes. CC Downgrader modifies the internal version headers in .aep and .aepx files so that older After Effects versions can open them smoothly.

Is this tool free and safe?

Yes, CC Downgrader is 100% free and open-source. Because conversions are handled entirely in your web browser, no data is uploaded or stored on external servers.

Which Adobe Creative Cloud versions are supported?

CC Downgrader supports all recent Adobe Premiere Pro (.prproj) and After Effects (.aep, .aepx) releases including CC 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, and legacy versions.
